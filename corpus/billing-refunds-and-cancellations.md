# Refunds, Credits and Cancellation

> Refunds are a finance-approved action. Support agents can explain this policy and can raise a
> refund request, but cannot issue a refund directly. All refund decisions are made by the billing
> team.

## Refund policy

- **Monthly plans.** Not refundable after the invoice is paid. The subscription can be cancelled to
  prevent the next charge.
- **Annual plans.** Refundable on a prorated basis within the first 30 days of the annual term. After
  day 30, no refund is issued; the term runs to its end date.
- **Overage charges.** Not refundable, except where an incident on Meridian's side caused the
  excess usage. These are handled case by case by the billing team.
- **Duplicate charges.** Always refunded in full once confirmed.
- **Taxes.** Refunded proportionally with the refunded amount.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 5–10 business days to appear,
depending on the issuing bank. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

## Account credits

Credits are applied to future invoices and cannot be converted to cash. Credits are issued for:

- prorated seat removals,
- prorated downgrades where an annual plan was paid up front,
- service credits under an Enterprise SLA,
- goodwill adjustments approved by the billing team.

Credits do not expire while the account is active. On account closure, unused credits are forfeited.

## Cancellation

Cancelling from Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription stops the next renewal. The account keeps
full access until the end of the paid period, then reverts to the Developer plan.

Cancellation does not delete data. To delete data, submit a deletion request separately — see the
data retention policy.

Enterprise contracts cannot be cancelled self-serve. They follow the termination clause in the
signed order form, which typically requires 60 days' written notice before the renewal date.

## Disputes and chargebacks

If a chargeback is filed, the account is immediately suspended pending resolution, and API access is
blocked for all keys. Resolving a chargeback in the customer's favour restores access within one
business day; resolving against restores access on payment of the disputed amount plus the
processor's dispute fee.

We ask customers to contact billing before filing a chargeback — nearly all disputes are resolved
faster directly.

## What support can do without finance approval

| Action | Support can do it |
|---|---|
| Explain the policy above | Yes |
| Send a copy of an invoice | Yes |
| Update the billing email or address | Yes |
| Raise a refund request to billing | Yes |
| Approve or issue a refund | No |
| Waive an overage charge | No |
| Extend a payment deadline | No — billing team only |
